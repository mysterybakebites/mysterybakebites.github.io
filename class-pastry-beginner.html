Overview

About this class

Learn the essential techniques for making popular Ghanaian pastries and snacks.

Recommendation: Start with Basic if you are new to pastry or want a strong foundation in popular Ghanaian pastries and snacks.

Is this class for you?

  • You are new to popular Ghanaian pastries and snacks
  • You want to practise dough, shaping and baking techniques
  • You want to recreate familiar pastries at home

Recommended level: No experience needed. A recommended starting point for new pastry learners.

Frequently asked

How do I pay?+

After we confirm your class date and available space, you can pay in cash or make full payment upfront through Mobile Money. We share MoMo details on WhatsApp.

Can I book more than one class?+

Yes. You can request more than one class. We confirm the combined class details and total with you on WhatsApp.

Is this the right level for me?+

No experience needed. A recommended starting point for new pastry learners. Not sure? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.
